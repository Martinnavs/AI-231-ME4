Branch `w/249`, worktree `~/AI-231-ME4-w249`.

## What was built

- `eval/sufficiency/implicit_compound.jsonl` — 60 rows (JSON per line) of
  implicit-compound sufficiency questions over the 44-document corpus at
  `/tmp/ev218/raw44`. Each question is phrased the way a person asks aloud
  (6-22 words, ends in `?`, no file names, no "document(s)/file(s)/notes",
  no "web/internet", no joined questions). None of them says "document + web";
  the compound is implicit in that the document holds one half or a dated half
  of the answer.
- `scripts/check_implicit_set.py` — corpus-agnostic validator (CLI:
  `--set`, `--root`, `--skip-evidence`, plus `--expected-counts`/`--min-files`
  overrides). Checks row count, kind balance, unique ids, required keys,
  question style (word count, terminal `?`, forbidden source words),
  `web_needed`/`web_fact` consistency per kind, `stale_marker` presence on
  `needs_web_stale`, evidence length, and — by default — that each row's
  `evidence` snippet occurs verbatim in the cited corpus file's extracted text
  (plain read for md/txt/csv/json; markitdown for docx/pptx/xlsx/pdf/html;
  rows are flagged, not crashed on, when no parser is available).
- `tests/test_check_implicit_set.py` — 15 tests over a tiny synthetic 3-file
  corpus (2 folders, plain text): clean pass, evidence-not-found, skip-evidence
  behaviour, duplicate id, forbidden word, word count, missing doc_file, wrong
  kind counts, web_fact/stale_marker consistency, non-question, too few
  distinct files, JSONL round-trip, and the CLI pass/fail paths.

## Design notes

- **stale (20)**: the doc holds a dated value (FY2026 per diem, 2026 GS table,
  2026/2025 IRS rates, July 2024 population vintage, August 2026 EIA prices,
  September 2026 ONS GVA, 2025 budget bill, proposed/2026-27 academic
  calendars) and the question is in the present/next period ("right now",
  "next year", "this fiscal year"). Time anchor: today is late November 2026.
- **missing_half (15)**: the doc supplies exactly one half (a city, a company
  name, an agency identity, a state) and the spoken answer quietly needs a
  second fact that lives only on the web (time zone, capital, headquarters,
  current office holder, distance, next decennial census date).
- **conflict (10)**: the doc states something that may have changed — an
  interim coordinator, a "Tentative" fall break, a proposed exam week, the
  2020 WOTUS definition, an "at this time" laptop stock note, the 2024 state
  ranking, a Jul-Sep 2026 survey, 2020 module instructors, a Sep-2026 growth
  reading, a 2025 office location.
- **controls (15)**: fully answerable from the documents and current enough,
  deliberately including items that *look* time-sensitive (a "right now"
  salary from the table currently in force, "this year's" mileage rate, a
  2026 tax deadline stated in a 2025 instructions file, a settled 2020-2024
  state gain) so over-triggering is measurable.
- Every `doc_fact` is backed by a verbatim `evidence` snippet (<= 200 chars)
  verified to occur in the file's markitdown extraction; the validator re-runs
  that check against the real corpus.

## Rows I was unsure about

- **imp-057** ("How much does a GS-12 step 5 make right now?", control): kept
  as a control because the 2026 GS table is the schedule in force as of
  November 2026, but it becomes stale in January 2027 — the control decays if
  the experiment runs much later.
- **imp-044** ("Is the UK economy still growing?"): classed as conflict rather
  than stale (the doc's last reading, Sep 2026, is recent; the issue is that
  growth may have stopped since). Borderline; both readings are defensible.
- **imp-017** (NM court budget): the appropriation column's units are not
  stated in the extraction, so the row quotes the raw entry "6000 (the bill's
  units)" instead of asserting $6M.
- **imp-010** (EIA year-to-date total): the YTD figure 3,073,105 is verified,
  but that the monthly series stops at August 2026 is read off the monthly
  rows, not a stated coverage note.
- **imp-028** (NACA report): the original publisher is not stated in the
  extracted text (the author line is OCR-garbled), so the row's web_fact
  points at the NTRS record rather than asserting NACA as fact.
- **imp-001/imp-002** (per diem): rely on FY2026 ending 2025-10-01 and the
  current fiscal year being FY2027 — true as of the ticket date.
