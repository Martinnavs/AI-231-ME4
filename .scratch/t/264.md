# Ticket 264 (agy neon, RESEARCH/ideation): can ME4 be measured with the FRAMES / MMLongBench-Doc / MiMoTable benchmark protocol on the Raspberry Pi, with pure-audio speed?

## Context
ME4 is a voice+text agent on a Raspberry Pi 4 (VAD on the Pi, STT/TTS external on a laptop, router kept, agent + MCP servers on the Pi). It answers only from a user's zip of mixed documents, the web, or both. The production test is PURE AUDIO. Read first: docs/ME4-RELEASE-MANIFEST-2026-11-27.md, docs/ME4-POSTDEPLOY-STATE-2026-11-27.md, docs/ME4-HANDOFF-2026-11-26.md (sections 14-18), .scratch/pi-check/REPORT5.md..REPORT7.md if present, scripts/judge_eval.py (Qwen logprob judge), scripts/talk.sh and scripts/stack/*.sh (how the Pi is driven), pipeline/src/voice_pipeline/ (agent_server.py jsonl turn logs), and how the earlier Pi runs were scored.
The owner wants an evaluation protocol like this (verbatim):
---
Measured for every dataset
Correctness: each dataset's official scorer, or a fixed AI judge where there isn't one.
Speed: time from the end of the spoken question to the first audio of the real answer. Filler like "one moment", plans and clarifying questions don't stop the clock. Reported as p50 and p95.
Also logged: time to the full answer, how often the agent asks a clarifying question, speech recognition errors, and the Pi's CPU, RAM and temperature.
FRAMES (824 questions, Wikipedia): multi-document reasoning, each question needs 2 to 15 articles; numbers, dates and timelines, comparisons, tabular facts, post-processing such as math or counting. Reported: accuracy from an AI judge, also by how many articles a question needs. A significant portion of the articles are converted to docx, html, txt or odt, so file reading is tested too.
MMLongBench-Doc (1,082 questions, 135 long PDFs): understanding one long PDF, answers spread across pages. Evidence types: text, tables, charts, figures and layout; 26% of pages are image-only and need OCR or a vision model. 223 questions have no answer (tests saying "not found" instead of guessing). Reported: official accuracy and F1, plus whether the cited page is correct.
MiMoTable (641 questions, 206 real .xlsx files): spreadsheets with multiple sheets, merged cells and complicated headers; lookup, calculation, comparison, reasoning; 50 questions need 2 to 4 files. Reported: accuracy from an AI judge, also by table type and operation, plus whether the cited file is correct.
---
Some datasets are being checked by classmates; if need be, MMLongBench-Doc is priority 1.
